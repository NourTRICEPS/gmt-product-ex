🛒 GMT Product Extractor

Collect every product from any shop website, filter it, and download it as Excel

Method 1 — Bookmark button (easiest)

  1. Show the bookmarks bar in Chrome: Ctrl + Shift + B
  2. Drag the button below onto the bookmarks bar:

    🛒 GMT Products
  3. Open a product listing page (for example the TV category on Mytek or Tunisianet), then click the button on your bookmarks bar.
  4. A panel appears and automatically collects all the pages (no further click needed). Wait for "Done", then filter (brand, price, deals…) and download Excel.

The panel is in English; the FR/AR/EN button switches language.

Method 2 — No bookmark (Console)

  1. Open the product listing page, press F12, then open the Console tab.
  2. Click the button below, paste into the Console, and press Enter.
    (If Chrome asks, type allow pasting first.)

Notes

  • The tool only works on the site you are on, and the data stays in your browser (nothing is sent to any server).
  • If a page is not detected, send us the page link and we will tune it.
  • Columns: Source, Category, Brand, SKU, Product, Price, Old price, Discount %, Availability, URL, Image, Date.
